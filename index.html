Quick answers

What does this cost me?
Nothing. Communities pay locator fees when you sign a lease — the same budget they use for advertising. You'll never get a bill from us.
How is this different from the review pages?
Reviews tell you how residents feel after move-in. They can't tell you what a unit quotes this week, which specials are running, or how a property tends to screen applicants. That's the half we add.
How fast is the list?
Usually within 24 hours, sent to your phone and email. If you're on a tight timeline, say so and we'll move quicker.
I'm stationed at JBSA / moving on orders. Can you work around a report date?
All the time — PCS moves are half our job. Note your base and report date in the form and we'll pace the search to your arrival.
What happens after I get the list?
Heart your favorites and we take it from there — tours, applications, follow-up, all the way through move-in.
